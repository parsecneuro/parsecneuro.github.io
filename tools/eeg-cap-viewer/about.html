ToolsEEG Cap Viewer

Interactive browser application

See where the electrodes are—and how two caps differ.

The EEG Cap Viewer is a browser-based 3D workspace for inspecting standard electrode layouts, loading user coordinates, comparing matched electrodes, and reviewing the coordinate and broad cortical context of a selected location.

Launch the viewer

What to expect

A visual check before coordinates become analysis.

The viewer is intended to make electrode placement easier to inspect, communicate, and quality-check without requiring a desktop visualization package.

Standard layouts

10–20, 10–10, 10–5, and additional systems

Choose a bundled montage and display its electrode labels over the three-dimensional template scalp.

Your coordinates

Load a reference CSV

Import electrode labels and Cartesian coordinates, select the units, and preserve the source values while the display head adapts beneath them.

Cap comparison

Measure displacement

Load a second CSV, match electrodes by label, and inspect source-space and template-MNI displacement values.

Selective display

Show only what matters

Turn individual electrodes on or off so dense layouts can be simplified for inspection, teaching, figures, or quality review.

Electrode context

Select a point to inspect it

Click an electrode to view its original coordinate, template MNI/fsaverage projection, and concise functional context.

Local processing

Your uploaded data stays in the browser

CSV files are processed locally by the viewer and are not uploaded to or stored by the website.

A typical visit

From montage selection to a displacement table.

  1. 01

    Choose a system

    Start from the bundled layout that matches the intended cap or recording system.

  2. 02

    Load coordinates

    Use the built-in reference or upload a CSV containing electrode labels and Cartesian coordinates.

  3. 03

    Control the view

    Rotate the head, choose which electrodes appear, and select a location to inspect its coordinate information.

  4. 04

    Compare and export

    Add a second cap, examine matched-electrode displacement, and download the comparison table for later review.

Interpretation boundary

Designed for visualization and quality review.

Electrode-to-region descriptions and template-MNI projections are broad template-level approximations. The viewer is not a source-localization, neuronavigation, diagnostic, or participant-specific MRI coregistration system.
