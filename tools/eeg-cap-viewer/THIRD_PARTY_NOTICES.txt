MNE-Python
----------
The built-in montage coordinates and template scalp asset were prepared using
MNE-Python 1.11.0. International 10-20, 10-10, and 10-5 presets use selected
positions from MNE standard montage definitions. Additional presets include
alternative standard layouts and manufacturer-oriented template montages.

The browser head mesh contains arrays converted from the MNE-bundled
fsaverage-head.fif scalp/head surface. It is a public template surface and is
not an anatomical scan of a viewer or study participant.

Template interpretation
-----------------------
The template scalp and any estimated MNI/fsaverage coordinates are intended
for visualization and broad quality review. Native MNI/fsaverage montage
coordinates are used directly. These resources do not replace individual EEG
digitization, participant MRI coregistration, source localization,
neuronavigation, diagnosis, or clinical interpretation.
