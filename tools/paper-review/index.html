PAPER REVIEW / ALPHA 1

Your paper, on your device.

How to use it

Open a PDF. Read on the left and scroll to figures or other sections on the right. Drag the dividers to give either view or the notebook more room.

Start a comment to capture the current left-hand PDF page. Add it to your notebook, then use View all comments to edit your review. TXT export includes saved comments and any unfinished draft, without automatic page markers.

Mark up the PDF

Use the small toolbar above either PDF to highlight, add a note, or draw a circle or rectangle. Choose the arrow to select and edit a mark, or the eraser to remove it. Undo restores the last annotation change. Your marks appear in both views and stay aligned when you zoom.

Use Export PDF below the main notebook to keep a marked copy. Notebook comments are exported separately as plain text.

What stays local

Your PDF is read in browser memory and is not uploaded. Notes, PDF annotations, your unfinished comment, and extracted references are saved in this browser for that PDF. Reopen the same PDF to continue. The PDF itself is not saved by the app.

PDF viewing and English OCR use files bundled with the app. There are no AI review services or automatic reference searches. Optional Google links send the chosen citation only when you open them.

App opening counter

When the site counter is enabled, opening this page sends the app identifier and a random request ID to the counter service. Your PDF, filename, notes, annotations, and references are not sent. Local previews do not send counter requests.

Local saving

Export a TXT copy when you finish a session. Clearing browser data, private browsing, or changing browser or website address may remove or separate saved notes. Page markers refer to PDF page positions, which may differ from printed page numbers.

Reference extraction

Extraction supports common numbered and author–year bibliographies. Complex columns and scanned text can need correction. The reference finder uses local rules for common author–year and numbered styles. Choose the bibliography pages and, if needed, its citation style and column layout. Line-number removal is optional and starts off. The Original (v0.1) method restores the first version’s extraction. Use local OCR for scans and the editable bibliography field to correct remaining errors.

Export or remove a review

The buttons below the main notebook let you export your comments as TXT, export a marked PDF, or remove the current PDF and its saved review from this app. Removal includes comments, annotations, and references. Your original file stays on your computer.
